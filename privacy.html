The short version: your workouts, routes, steps, heart rate, and shoe details stay on your device. SoleSync has no accounts, no ads, and no tracking. What SoleSync is

SoleSync tracks the mileage on your running, walking, and hiking shoes against your workouts. It is a native iOS app for iPhone and iPad, iOS 17 or later. It has no accounts: there is nothing to sign up for and no password.

What SoleSync reads from Apple Health, and where it stays

With your permission, SoleSync reads the following from Apple Health (HealthKit):

  • Workouts — your runs, walks, and hikes, including distance, duration, and calories.
  • Workout routes — the GPS route of a workout, used to draw the map on a workout's detail screen.
  • Steps — used to calculate the average cadence shown for a workout.
  • Heart rate — the average heart rate shown for a workout and in your training totals.

This data is used on your device to match workouts to your shoes, calculate mileage, and build the metrics and insights you see in the app. SoleSync:

  • Never writes to Apple Health. It reads only; nothing is added, changed, or deleted in your health data.
  • Never sends your health data anywhere. Workouts, routes, steps, heart rates, shoe names, and exact distances never leave your device. They are not included in analytics and are not transmitted to us or to any third party. Two figures derived from this data do reach analytics, each only as a coarse band: the distance on a shoe when you retire it, and the number of workouts in an import. Both are described under What does leave your device.
  • Never reads health data you have not granted. You can decline Health access entirely and use SoleSync with manually entered workouts instead. You can also change or revoke access at any time in the Health app (Profile › Privacy › SoleSync) or under Settings › Privacy & Security › Health.

What does leave your device

SoleSync collects a small amount of diagnostic and usage information to keep the app working and to understand how it is used in aggregate. This is declared in the app's privacy manifest and matches the App Store privacy label, which reads Data Not Linked to You:

DataWhy it is collected
Crash dataTo find and fix crashes (crash reports from Firebase Crashlytics).
Other diagnostic dataTo diagnose failures and app stability (also from Crashlytics and its transport layer).
Product interactionAggregate counts of actions such as finishing onboarding or importing a workout, so we know which features are used.
Device IDA random identifier generated by the analytics SDK to distinguish one installation from another.

In plain terms:

  • None of this is linked to your identity. The app sets no user ID and collects no name, email, phone number, or contact information. The identifiers it uses are generated by the analytics SDK, not assigned to you, and the vendor-ID collection is disabled in the app.
  • None of this is used for tracking. There is no advertising identifier, no cross-app or cross-site tracking, and no App Tracking Transparency prompt.
  • There is no advertising in SoleSync, and your information is never sold or shared with data brokers, advertisers, or anyone else.

Usage analytics are bands and fixed categories only. Where an event carries a number, the app replaces the number with a band before sending it:

  • Retirement mileage band. When you retire a shoe, analytics receives which of four bands the shoe's total distance fell in: under 200 km, 200 to 500 km, 500 to 800 km, or over 800 km. The exact distance is not sent.
  • Workout import count band. When an import of workouts from Apple Health finishes, analytics receives how many workouts were imported, as one of five bands: 0, 1, 2 to 5, 6 to 20, or 21 or more. Nothing about the workouts themselves is sent.
  • Onboarding duration band. When you finish onboarding, analytics receives how long it took, as one of four bands: under 1 minute, 1 to 5 minutes, 5 to 15 minutes, or over 15 minutes.

The analytics system accepts no free-text values, so by design it cannot carry your shoe names, brands, workout content, routes, heart rates, or other health data.

Data retention and deletion

  • All of your SoleSync data — shoes, mileage, workout associations, and preferences — is stored locally on your device.
  • Deleting the app removes all of its local data. There is no server copy to erase because there is no server and no account.
  • Anything you granted from Apple Health remains in Apple Health; SoleSync never held a copy of it beyond your device, and revoking access removes SoleSync's ability to read it.
  • Crash and analytics data held by the analytics provider is retained under that provider's policies and contains no health data and no identity.

Children

SoleSync is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The app collects no personal information from anyone: there are no accounts, no contact details, and no free-text analytics.
