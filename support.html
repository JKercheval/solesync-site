Frequently asked questions

Do I need to give SoleSync access to Apple Health?

No. Health access lets SoleSync import your runs, walks, and hikes automatically, but you can decline it and enter workouts by hand instead. The app works either way.

My workouts aren't showing up. What should I check?
  • Make sure SoleSync has permission to read workouts: open the Health app and go to Profile › Privacy › SoleSync, or go to Settings › Privacy & Security › Health › SoleSync.
  • Check that the workout appears in the Health app itself. SoleSync can only import workouts that your watch or fitness app has saved to Apple Health.
  • Check that the shoe's auto-import setting is turned on for that activity type (running, walking, or hiking) in the shoe's settings.
Why don't I see a route map, cadence, or heart rate for a workout?

These come from Apple Health and are shown only when the workout includes them and you've allowed SoleSync to read workout routes, steps, and heart rate. Manually entered workouts don't include this data.

Does SoleSync change anything in Apple Health?

No. SoleSync only reads from Apple Health. It never adds, changes, or deletes your health data.

How do I retire a pair of shoes, or bring one back?

Retire a pair with a tap or a swipe; it moves to its own Retired section. You can bring a retired pair back at any time. Reaching the mileage limit doesn't retire a pair automatically, so you stay in control.

Can I use kilometers instead of miles?

Yes. Switch units in SoleSync's Settings.

Do I need an account? Where is my data stored?

There's no account. Your shoes, mileage, and preferences are stored on your device, and your health data never leaves it. See the Privacy Policy for details.

How do I delete my data?

Deleting the app removes all of its data from your device. There's no server copy to erase. Your workouts remain in Apple Health, which SoleSync never modifies.

Which devices are supported?

iPhone and iPad running iOS 17 or later.
