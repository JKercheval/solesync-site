Know when your shoes are worn out before your knees do.

SoleSync tracks the mileage on every pair of running, walking, and hiking shoes you own, matched against the workouts you already record in Apple Health.

Connect your workouts

Runs, walks, and hikes from Apple Health are assigned to the right pair automatically. Prefer not to share Health data? Enter workouts by hand.

Track every pair

Set a mileage limit for each pair and watch the distance add up. Retire a pair when it's done, and bring it back anytime.

Know when to replace

A 0–100 health score per pair combines mileage, daily wear rate, and consistency, with plain guidance on when to replace.

Private by design

Your health data never leaves your device. No account, no ads, and SoleSync never writes to Apple Health.

Requires iOS 17 or later. Works on iPhone and iPad.
